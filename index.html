Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about certifications, custom manufacturing, MOQs and export.

Do you manufacture custom or private-label gear? +

Yes. In-house designers and pattern masters develop products to buyer specifications — from adjusted webbing configurations to fully private-labelled ranges with your branding and packaging.

Which certifications and audits do you hold? +

Our quality system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and production is SEDEX-audited for ethical practices. SB International is also a Government of India recognised Export House. Product-level test documentation is shared with quotations on request.

Where do you export? +

The United States is our largest market, alongside customers worldwide. Export documentation, labelling and packing are all handled in-house.

What are your MOQs and lead times? +

Both vary by product and configuration. Send your requirement through the quote form and you'll receive MOQ, lead time and pricing together in one response.

How is quality controlled? +

Dedicated quality controllers inspect at every stage — incoming materials, in-process stitching and hardware, and finished goods — with stringent batch testing before dispatch.

How do I get the full catalogue? +

Email sales@sbfallprotection.com or submit the quote form and the team will send the latest edition with current specifications.
