## Two things to keep in mind

**Identifiable people.** Several Pexels photos show recognisable faces. Both the
Pexels and Mixkit licences forbid using such imagery in a way that *implies the
person endorses the product*. Using them as ambient scene photography is fine;
captioning one as an SB customer, or putting a testimonial quote against a stock
face, would not be. The reel captions were deliberately written to describe the
activity rather than claim it shows SB gear, for this reason.

**Placeholders still to replace.** The reel clips and the four testimonial
images are stock stand-ins. They should be swapped for SB's own footage before
launch — partly for licence comfort, mainly because real factory footage is more
persuasive than stock.
